# Course methods used
Sources are supplied SEEM3410 Week1_Teaching and Week2–6_Teaching PDFs (W. Liu, 2026–27 Term 1). Generic LectureNotes files in Downloads belong to SEEM3580 and were excluded.

| Stage | Course method | Actual use / limitation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Week 1 pp.6–13,16–21: abstraction, service-system entities/resources, discipline, performance and Little's law | Explicit two-stage scope; finite-run queue-area conservation rather than unsupported steady-state inference |
| 2 | Week 5 pp.4–14: scope/clean/explore; pp.32–35 empirical input; pp.52–61 time variation | ZIP audit, descriptive PMFs/ECDF, previous-clock gap model, exposure-based rates; no continuous KS p-value on discrete tied gaps |
| 3 | Week 2 pp.7–19 and 39–40: offered load, rho, limits of Erlang C | Capacity analysis only; priority/time-varying finite network violates exact M/M/c conditions |
| 4–5 | Week 3 pp.8–26: state, FEL, routines, clock, statistics, termination/ties; Week 4 heapq event loop | Pure event-driven doctor simulation and separate exact downstream FCFS availability calendar; hand trace, resource and area invariants, all-source replay |
| 6 | Week 3 random input generation; Week 4 RNG; Week 5 empirical calibration | SeedSequence independent replications/four substreams; inverse CDF, boundary-correct integrated-hazard alternative |
| 7–8 | Week 6 pp.4–11: decision levers, independent replications, CRN and paired differences; Week 4 output CIs | Shared patient tables across c; run-level inference; Student-t finite-sample refinement to approximate normal CI |
| 9 | Week 5 time-varying arrivals; Week 6 pp.12–20: factorial/paired interactions | One-factor sensitivities and paired difference-in-differences from existing cells; demand/service uncertainty distinguished from staffing decisions |
| 10 | Week 1 decision impact; Week 6 enumeration/confirmation p.46 | Minimum-cost feasible candidate computed; fresh seed confirmation, no unnecessary metamodel for three policies |
| 11–12 | Project Guidelines pp.1–3 | Seven sections, <=8 A4 pages, >=11 pt, >=2cm margins; separate code/data ZIP, 10-minute presentation |
