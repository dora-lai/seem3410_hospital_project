# Assumptions and provenance
All patient records are synthetic. The Kaggle link comes from the current hospital proposal; source generator and dataset license were not supplied or independently retrieved. archive.zip is preserved byte for byte. No records are dropped or edited. Latest proposal is SEEM3410_Project_Proposal (1).docx; the unnumbered proposal concerns Café 330 and is superseded. No hospital starter ZIP or existing hospital Python implementation was found; keyword hits in other scripts were unrelated interview documents.

Estimated from the 50 baseline runs: priority PMF, doctor/pharmacy duration PMFs, two arrival-gap PMFs conditional on previous arrival clock, two Poisson rates. Common service distributions are provisional, supported by descriptive dependence checks (see outputs/data/dependence.csv); absence of large differences is not proof of independence. Priority/arrival/service streams are independent in fresh generation; tails outside empirical support are unavailable.

Empirical renewal convention: first previous time=0; previous clock<=peak uses peak gap PMF, otherwise late; sampled gaps may cross boundary. No extra rounding. Discrete durations in base; scaled gaps/durations in sensitivities are continuous. Poisson alternative consumes integrated hazard at boundary correctly. Rate estimates use peak count/(R*240) and late count/sum(max(last arrival-240,0)); late exposures end at a fixed-count stopping time. Fixed 200 truncation limits interpretation of late hourly counts. Arrival-rate perturbations compress or expand a fixed cohort, not increase patients in a fixed shift.

Priority 3 highest; doctor queue key (-priority,arrival,PatientID); all arrivals at a timestamp precede doctor completions; simultaneous arrivals processed in PatientID order; completions deterministic insertion sequence. Pharmacy receives doctor completions sorted (DocEnd,PatientID); no blocking feedback, so a separate availability calendar is exact for this network. Available resource chosen by earliest availability then ID. These are reconstruction conventions, not documented Kaggle generator rules.

Illustrative target 15 (also 10/20/30), horizon 1080, wage 100, overtime premium 1/1.5/2; not clinical standards, real shifts or payroll. Staffing stays to drain. Scheduled cost positive and proportional to doctor count; wage alone cannot change its ranking. One or two pharmacy counters is only a performance sensitivity, with no assumed pharmacy wage. 50 independent replications preselected as checklist minimum; confidence intervals/independent confirmation assess precision, uncertain cells remain explicitly uncertain. No optimization by changing targets after observing results.

Team names/IDs copied from supplied hospital proposal; team must confirm. AI assisted implementation, execution, figures and report writing; team must review and understand work and determine the required declaration. No submission or rehearsal is performed by software.

Numerical resolution: time_decimals=9 (configurable). Generated inputs and service completion clocks are rounded to 1e-9 minute before FEL ordering. This prevents arithmetic noise turning intended simultaneous arrivals/completions into different events under fractional sensitivity factors. Validation tolerance is 1e-8 minute. Original integer source inputs are unchanged. A diagnostic from the initial implementation is retained in outputs/verification/float_tie_diagnostic.csv; the false eligibility assertion prompted this general numerical fix, not a patient-specific patch.
